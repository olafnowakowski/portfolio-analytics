# BigQuery Account Types

## Main Idea

BigQuery can be used to view and analyze data from many sources.

For this course, you can use BigQuery at no charge. The two main no-charge options discussed are:

1. **BigQuery Sandbox**
2. **Google Cloud Free Trial**

---

# What Is BigQuery?

**BigQuery** is a Google Cloud tool used to view, query, and analyze data.

Data Analysts can use BigQuery to practice SQL and work with large datasets.

---

# BigQuery No-Charge Options

BigQuery offers ways to use the tool without paying.

The lesson focuses on:

- sandbox account
- free trial account

Paid options are available, but they are not needed for the course activities.

---

# BigQuery Sandbox

## Definition

A **BigQuery Sandbox account** is a no-charge BigQuery account that anyone with a Google account can use.

It does not require payment information.

---

# Advantages of BigQuery Sandbox

The sandbox is useful because:

- it is available at no charge
- anyone with a Google account can access it
- no payment method is required
- it works for most course activities
- it lets learners practice BigQuery and SQL

---

# BigQuery Sandbox Limitations

The sandbox account has some limitations.

Examples include:

- a maximum of 12 projects at a time
- no support for some Data Manipulation Language operations
- limited ability to insert or update database records

---

# 12 Project Limit

With a sandbox account, you can have a maximum of **12 projects** at one time.

If you want to create a 13th project, you must delete one of the original 12 projects.

---

# DML Operations

## Definition

**DML** stands for **Data Manipulation Language**.

DML operations are actions that change data in a database.

Examples include:

- inserting new records
- updating existing field values
- deleting records

---

# DML Limitation in BigQuery Sandbox

The BigQuery sandbox does not allow you to:

- insert new records into a database
- update field values in existing records

These DML operations are not supported in the sandbox.

---

# Why Sandbox Is Still Useful

Even with limitations, the sandbox works for most course activities.

The course does not require inserting new records or updating existing records.

---

# Google Cloud Free Trial

## Definition

The **Google Cloud Free Trial** gives users access to more Google Cloud and BigQuery features with fewer limitations than the sandbox.

---

# Free Trial Credit

The Google Cloud Free Trial provides **$300 in credit** to use in Google Cloud during the first **90 days**.

---

# Free Trial Time Limit

The free trial expires after:

- the $300 credit is used
- or 90 days pass

Whichever happens first ends the trial.

---

# Free Trial and BigQuery Practice

If you only use the BigQuery console to practice SQL queries, you are unlikely to get close to the $300 spending limit.

---

# Payment Information Requirement

The free trial requires you to set up a payment option with Google Cloud.

However, your payment method will not be automatically charged after the trial ends.

---

# What Happens When the Free Trial Ends?

After the free trial ends, you must personally choose to upgrade to a paid account if you want to continue using paid Google Cloud services.

Google Cloud will not automatically charge your payment method unless you opt in to upgrade.

---

# Why Some Learners Avoid the Free Trial

Some learners may not feel comfortable entering payment information.

This is one reason the BigQuery sandbox exists.

The sandbox lets users practice without providing payment details.

---

# Sandbox vs Free Trial

| Feature | BigQuery Sandbox | Google Cloud Free Trial |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | No charge | No charge during trial |
| Payment method required | No | Yes |
| Credit | Not based on credit | $300 credit |
| Time limit | No 90-day trial limit mentioned | 90 days |
| Limitations | More limitations | Fewer limitations |
| DML support | Limited or not supported | More features available |
| Best for course activities | Yes | Optional |

---

# Upgrading to a Paid Account

With either account type, you can upgrade to a paid account at any time.

If you upgrade, you can retain your existing projects.

---

# If You Do Not Upgrade After Free Trial

If you set up a free trial account and choose not to upgrade after the trial ends, you can set up a free sandbox account at that time.

---

# Which Account Should You Use?

For most course activities, the **BigQuery Sandbox** is enough.

The **Google Cloud Free Trial** gives access to more features, but it requires payment information.

---

# Big Lesson

BigQuery can be used at no charge through either the sandbox or the free trial.

The sandbox is simpler and does not require payment information, while the free trial has fewer limitations but requires a payment method.

# Final Summary

This lesson explains two no-charge ways to use BigQuery: the BigQuery Sandbox and the Google Cloud Free Trial. The BigQuery Sandbox is free, available to anyone with a Google account, and does not require payment information. It has limitations, including a maximum of 12 projects and no support for some Data Manipulation Language operations, such as inserting or updating records.
