### Fill in the blank: Margin of error is the _____ amount that the sample results are expected to differ from those of the actual population. 
- maximum
> Margin of error is the maximum amount that the sample results are expected to differ from those of the actual population.

### In a survey about a new gardening product, 80% of respondents report they would buy the product again. The margin of error for the survey is 5%. Based on that margin of error, what range reflects the population's true response? 
- 75-85%
> Based on the margin of error, a range of 75-85% would accurately reflect the population’s true response.
